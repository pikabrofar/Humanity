ojoS

The cursor goes where you look.

A calibrated gaze cursor from the webcam you already have, built on Apple's Vision framework. No extra hardware.

2–5°

Fig. 1.0 · Fixation stabilizer Holding

Try it with your pointer.Drag a finger across the box. We add webcam-like jitter to its position. The ring stays put until several samples agree on a new spot, then a damped spring carries it there.

ojoS1 / 4

  1. Calibrate once. Follow nine dots with your eyes. Each dot waits for a steady fixation, and a point you miss comes back at the end.

  2. Then follow a moving dot. Its Lissajous path gives hundreds of distinct targets in 16 seconds. A short head-motion pass comes last.

  3. It holds still while you read. The cursor rests during a fixation and jumps when your eyes move. Dwell to click, or press ⌃⌥⌘G to click where you look.

  4. See where you looked. Press ⌥⌘R to record a session, then open its heatmap and scanpath, or export PNG and CSV.

manoS

A trackpad in the air.

Point with your palm and pinch to click. Apple's Vision finds the 21 joints of your hand in every frame, on your Mac.

Joints per frame
21
Engage delay
250 ms
Click rewind
About 100 ms

manoS1 / 5

  1. Open your hand and move. Pointing is relative, like a trackpad. Slow movements are precise and fast ones cross the screen.

  2. Pinch to click. Hold to drag. Thumb and index. Two quick pinches double-click, and a held pinch becomes a drag once you move past a small distance.

  3. Pinch your middle finger to right-click. Thumb and middle. Hold the pinch and move to scroll.

  4. Make a fist to hold the pointer. Reposition your hand while the pointer stays put, then open it and carry on.

  5. Flick a V for next or previous. Hold up two fingers and flick up or down to move between videos, pages and slides.

bocaS1 / 4

  1. Hold ⌃⌥⌘D and talk. A small pill near the bottom of the screen shows your level and the words as they're recognized.

  2. Release, and it's typed. Clean text lands at your cursor in any app. Fillers, stutters, punctuation and capitals are fixed on your Mac.

  3. Notes that summarize themselves. Record a voice note for a summary with action items. Search, play back and export to Markdown from the Library.

  4. Meetings, with names. Speakers are separated on your Mac and labeled in the transcript. Every recording asks you to confirm that everyone agreed.

Safeguards

Clicks only when you mean them.

Webcam tracking can misread you, so sentidoS is careful about acting on it.

  1. manoS

    Engage delay

    Nothing happens until a hand has been steady in view for 250 ms, so a hand passing by does nothing.

  2. manoS

    Click rewind

    Pinching moves your hand a little. Each click lands where the pointer was about 100 ms before the pinch began.

    −100 ms
  3. manoS

    Your mouse wins

    Touching the real mouse or trackpad pauses hand input for 1.5 s, so the two never fight.

    1.5 s
  4. manoS

    A kill switch

    ⌃⌥⌘H turns hand control off from anywhere. It never resumes on launch, and held buttons are released if camera frames stall.

    ⌃⌥⌘H
  5. ojoS

    Dwell starts off

    Dwell clicking is off on each launch and avoids dialogs and close buttons. ⌃⌥⌘E toggles it, and Esc cancels a dwell.

  6. bocaS

    Password fields stay private

    Dictation is never sent, cleaned up or saved while macOS reports a password field.

    ••••••
  7. bocaS

    No silent recording

    Meetings show a banner that can't be closed and a menu bar indicator. There's no silent all-system-audio fallback.

    Rec

Specifications

The details.

Requirements
macOS 14 Sonoma or later on Apple silicon. A built-in or external webcam and a microphone.
Eye tracking
Vision face landmarks, gradient-based pupil centers and a geometric gaze model. Calibration takes about 45 s: a 9-point grid, a 16 s smooth-pursuit target, a head-motion pass and 5 validation points. Typically within 2–5°.
Hand tracking
Vision hand pose with 21 joints, smoothed with a 1€ filter. Pinch thresholds are fitted to your hand. 250 ms engage delay and about 100 ms click rewind.
Speech
SpeechAnalyzer and SpeechTranscriber on macOS 26, and on-device SFSpeechRecognizer on macOS 14–15. No cloud recognition.
Cleanup and summaries
Apple Foundation Models when Apple Intelligence is on, otherwise deterministic rules. Optional bring-your-own-key providers: Groq, Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter and Ollama.
Meetings
Mic and call audio through a process tap, with a ScreenCaptureKit fallback. On-device speaker separation, optional voice profiles with consent.
Privacy
No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or update checks. Voice profiles are deleted after 12 months unused.
License and price
Free and open source under the MIT License. Nothing is locked, and an optional donation changes nothing.

Safe use

sentidoS is general-purpose input software, not a medical or assistive device. Keep a keyboard and mouse or trackpad available, and don't rely on it for anything urgent or safety-critical. Webcam tracking is approximate and can misread you.

Recording a call or saving someone's voiceprint may require their consent by law. The app asks you to confirm before each recording.
