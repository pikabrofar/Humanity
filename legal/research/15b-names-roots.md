## Top 5 (best first)

1. **Embodra** has no conflicts anywhere in the screen, both .com and .app look free, and the word is easy to say (em-BOD-ra). It carries the idea of embodied or in-body interaction, which suits eyes, hands and voice together. It reads as an "embody" derivative rather than a classical-root word, so a lawyer should confirm it is not too close to "Embodia/Embodi" marks in health tech. Module names would be Embodra Eyes, Hands and Voice.
2. **Tactuva** (tac-TOO-va) is built on the Latin root for touch and has no web, GitHub or domain conflicts. The touch root fits hand gestures best, so the voice and gaze modules rely on the house mark alone. Mild risk of being misread as "Tactiva". Module names would be Tactuva Gaze, Gesture and Dictate.
3. **Synaesa** (sy-NAY-sa, or sy-NEE-sa) comes from the Greek for combined senses, which fits a suite that bridges eyes, hands and voice. It has no conflicts and free domains. It is the weakest on spelling and pronunciation, so test it aloud.
4. **Aisthea** has the best meaning (perception, aisthesis), free domains and only hobby-level GitHub use. It is hard to pronounce and spell ("aes-" and "aist-" confusion), and "sens" is avoided but this drifts toward "aesthetic."
5. **Somavo** (SO-ma-vo) is body-rooted and easy to say, but it sits in a crowded "Soma" neighbourhood (Soma Novo, Soma Software, the SomaVoice repo) and somavo.com is already registered. Use it only if the first four are blocked by a formal search.

Avoid: Cheirio (CheirOS overlap), Kinora, Praesio, Somatra, Haptora, Tangira, Tactora.

Next steps for the winners: search Embodra and Tactuva directly in the USPTO database (classes 9, 42, 38), check EUIPO/TMview, App Store search and Product Hunt, confirm that `embodra.com` or `tactuva.com` can actually be registered, and check pronunciation in Spanish, French, German and Mandarin.
