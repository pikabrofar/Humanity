## Top 5 (least bad)
1. **Quarro** (KWAR-oh). No exact software hit in App Store or GitHub; distinctive "qu" plus doubled r; .app appears unregistered. Concerns: a live quarro.com business (field unknown, check it) and Quaro analytics software nearby. Modules: Quarro Gaze / Gesture / Dictate.
2. **Dravi** (DRAH-vee). No same-name software; .app appears unregistered; short and easy to say. Concerns: dravi.com is a German IT-services-type firm (class 42-adjacent), and Draiv/Drivi apps are similar-sounding. Check the German registers.
3. **Sevro** (SEV-roh). Only a pre-release GitHub repo of the exact name; no app-store product. Concerns: .com is taken, Severa/Sevora nearby, and sev- sounds like "Servo". Risk could drop if the .com owner is unrelated.
4. **Mivro**. Exact-name app exists but is a small hobby/hackathon barcode-scanner project in a different field (food/health), archived repo. Weaker pick: same classes (cl. 9 mobile app), so a conflict is plausible.
5. **Kelvo / Tavro** (tie, weak). Both have live same-class software owners (invoicing SaaS; AI-governance platform) in different fields. Listed only as fallbacks; I would not use either without a full search.

Recommendation: do not stop at this list. All twelve are taken or crowded in some form, so a second brainstorm round with rarer letter combinations (x, z, q, w, j clusters, or unusual endings) is advisable. First run a formal USPTO/EUIPO search on Quarro and Dravi.
